# Storyboard Sheet 固定提示词

A grid-based storyboarding sheet, presented as a black and white sketch composition, featuring multiple sequential panels (e.g., 3x2 or 4x2 grid). Each panel depicts key narrative moments using high-contrast, black ink line illustrations, charcoal textures, and expressive linework, rendered exclusively in monochrome. The overall sheet, however, is overlaid with distinct, hand-drawn vibrant color annotations (reds, blues, greens, yellows). These colorful additions include arrows, circles, handwritten notes specifying camera movements (e.g., "TRACKING SHOT", "HIGH ANGLE"), lens type ("WIDE-ANGLE"), dialogue, and character expressions, cutting across and surrounding the black and white panels. The entire sheet has the authentic feel of a dynamic, working document from a production design meeting, combining raw artistic draftsmanship with detailed technical instruction, focusing mainly on the dynamics and composition, not detailed illustrations.

Determine the appropriate number of sequential panels and grid layout automatically based on the duration, narrative density, action changes, and camera changes of this storyboard sequence. Use enough panels to clearly communicate every essential beat without overcrowding the sheet. Arrange all panels in clear chronological reading order from left to right and top to bottom.
