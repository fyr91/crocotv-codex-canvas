# 四视图固定基础提示词

> `templateKey: croco.p3.character-four-view`
> `templateVersion: 1.0.0`

Create one single production character reference sheet with exactly four views. On the left, place one large waist-up hero portrait showing the face, silhouette, clothing construction, colors, and material details clearly. On the right, arrange exactly three orthographic full-body views in this order: complete front view, complete left-side profile view, complete back view. Keep exactly the same character identity, facial structure, body proportions, clothing, accessories, colors, materials, scale, eye line, and ground line across all four views. The three full-body views must show the entire body from head to feet without cropping. Use a clean neutral background. No extra views, characters, poses, outfits, props, text, arrows, labels, logos, panel numbers, borders, or watermarks. Treat this as the canonical structural and material reference for later production.

在固定结构之后追加当前角色总体设定、Variation（如有）和项目视觉风格。固定部分不限制媒介风格，但不得改变四区数量、位置和一致性要求。
